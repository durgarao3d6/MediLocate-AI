Common questions

What to know before testing

Is this free to try?

The current browser pilot is accessible without registration or payment. It is a development prototype, not a production subscription offering.

Do I need Android Studio or an app installation?

No. Use the hosted browser pilot. Android native SQLite and device testing are not complete.

Can I upload photos of medicine racks?

Yes, the prototype uses English OCR to suggest text. Every result needs manual verification; crowded rack photographs may produce incorrect or incomplete results.

Will it read handwritten prescriptions?

Handwriting and multilingual recognition are not validated. Start with synthetic printed English prescriptions or manual text entry.

Where is my information stored?

In this browser's IndexedDB storage, not native SQLite. There is no account recovery, cloud backup or cross-device synchronization in the pilot.

Can I use it for real dispensing?

No. It is a synthetic-data usability prototype. A qualified pharmacist must independently verify all medicine names, strengths and formulations. The app does not decide substitutions or stock availability.

Prototype safety: Do not upload real patient prescriptions, personal information or production pharmacy inventory. OCR and matching can be wrong. This tool is not approved for clinical or dispensing decisions.
